  Several assemblies were built from multiple components and needed secondary operations to put
  them together. The goal was lower cost while keeping fit, function, and manufacturability at
  volume.
outcome: >-
  The simplified single-part designs eliminated the secondary operations.

- **Design.** Took components and assemblies from concept through production, with 3D models and
  2D drawings in SolidWorks and Creo.
- **Tolerancing.** Applied GD&T and tolerance stack-up analysis so the consolidated part still
  fits and functions at volume.
- **Reviews.** Led cross-functional design reviews with manufacturing, quality, and suppliers,
  and tracked action items to closure.
- **Release.** Managed design release and revision control through ECO/ECN.
- **Suppliers.** Reviewed and dispositioned non-conforming components with suppliers and updated
  designs where needed.

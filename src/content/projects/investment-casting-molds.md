- **Molds.** Designed and fabricated the investment casting molds.
- **Thermal process.** Managed the full cycle from melt through solidification and
  post-processing.
- **Inspection.** Validated part accuracy with GD&T and dimensional inspection.
- **Defects.** Analyzed porosity and shrinkage and redesigned mold geometry to remove the root
  causes.
- **Iteration.** Adjusted tooling and process parameters to improve quality, consistency, and
  dimensional conformance across runs.
- **Documentation.** Wrote process documentation, control plans, and PFMEAs using Lean
  Manufacturing principles.

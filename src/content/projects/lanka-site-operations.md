- **Cleanup.** Cut and removed scrap steel, loaded dumpsters, and operated forklifts, vehicles,
  and heavy machinery.
- **Organization.** Organized storage areas, cleaned the workshop and tents, and identified
  reusable inventory.
- **Fabrication.** Built structural supports for the museum project, plus fixtures and welding
  setups.
- **Maintenance.** Repaired equipment, troubleshot machines, and organized tools.
- **Triage.** Every item had to be sorted: repair, store, recycle, or discard. Prioritizing that
  flow was the real work.

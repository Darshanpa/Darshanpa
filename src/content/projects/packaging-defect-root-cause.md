- **Root cause.** Ran RCA and Shainin RED X investigations on product and manufacturing issues,
  documented the findings, and put corrective actions in place to prevent recurrence. I am
  Shainin RED X Apprentice certified.
- **Redesign.** Addressed sealing, wear, and interface failures with component-level redesigns.
- **Validation.** Proved each change with prototypes and lab testing: lifecycle, vibration, drop,
  and thermal. Designs were iterated against real failure modes, not only simulation output.
- **Statistics.** Used DOE, SPC, Cp/Cpk, and regression in Minitab to quantify process
  performance and confirm that a change worked.
- **Risk.** Built DFMEAs and PFMEAs to catch failure modes before committing to production
  tooling.

  list for each day and a list of what to make ahead for the whole week.
- **Procurement by list.** The shopping list was split by store with a quantity for every item,

- **Constraints.** A separate menu for the Ekadashi fasting day, and packed lunches for the days
  spent hiking.
- **Bulk recipes.** Step breakdowns for the dishes cooked at volume, such as sheet pizza, panini,
  and street tacos.
- **Execution.** We arrived, learned the kitchen, and cooked the plan.

- **Leadership.** Led the sub-team through scoping, design, validation, and delivery.
- **Design.** Modeled the system in SolidWorks, checked it with FEA in ANSYS, and prepared parts
  for manufacture with CAM and GD&T.
- **Validation.** Tested on the car with data acquisition systems and physical testing.
- **Reviews.** Facilitated design reviews with more than 12 cross-functional engineers on a team
  of over 40.
- **Beyond brakes.** Also contributed as an ergonomics and aero team member.
